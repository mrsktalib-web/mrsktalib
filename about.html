Silence Is My Comfort

I enjoy silence and peaceful surroundings. I especially like quiet nights, moonlight and moments when I can think without disturbance. Loud noise and very bright lights are not my preference.

My Strength: I try to understand other people's feelings. I do not like making anyone feel insulted or uncomfortable. I like helping others.


My Weakness: Sometimes I trust people too quickly and consider others very close to me.

I believe in treating people with respect. I want to help others without making them feel small. Everyone deserves happiness, peace and freedom.
