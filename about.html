A FEW WORDS ABOUT ME

About Myself

I like to think about things in my own way. I value peace, personal freedom and a life where people can live happily without hurting one another. I believe that how we treat others matters a lot.

THINGS I LIKE

My Interests

  • Technology and computers
  • Learning new things and gaining knowledge
  • Thinking deeply and exploring ideas
  • Peaceful natural places
  • Spending quiet time by myself
MY NATURE

My Personality

I like being alone, especially at night. I enjoy silence and peaceful surroundings. I do not like loud noise or harsh brightness. A quiet night under the moonlight makes me feel comfortable.

I do not always want the same atmosphere. My mood matters. Sometimes I like to sit quietly and think; sometimes I simply want to enjoy the surroundings.

KNOWING MYSELF

My Strength and Weakness

My Strength

I try to understand how the other person might feel. I do not want my words or actions to make someone feel bad, awkward or insulted. I like helping people and want everyone to feel happy and free.

My Weakness

I trust people quickly and often consider others as my own. Sometimes I give people more trust than I should.

WHAT I BELIEVE

My Philosophy

I believe in treating people with respect. I want to help others without making them feel small. I like the idea of a world where everyone can live happily, peacefully and freely.

"Help others, respect their feelings, and let everyone live freely."
PEACEFUL LIVING

My Nature and Lifestyle

I feel comfortable in quiet natural places. I like sitting under a tree, near a pond or beside a river, especially in the afternoon. I prefer peaceful surroundings over crowded and noisy places.

I am not particularly fond of travelling. I enjoy the calmness of a place more than the rush of going from one place to another.

MOMENTS I ENJOY

My Favourite Moments

I enjoy different moments according to my mood. Quiet afternoons under a tree, the view beside a pond or river, peaceful evenings and moonlit nights are some of the moments I appreciate.

A PEACEFUL DREAM

My Dream Lifestyle

I like the idea of a peaceful life close to nature, with a calm home, trees, open space and animals around. A simple and natural environment feels more meaningful to me than a noisy lifestyle.
